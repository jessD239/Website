# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

## 1. Introduction
We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or subscribe to our newsletter. 

We comply with the New Zealand **Privacy Act 2020** and the **Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007**.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes:
* **Email Address:** Collected when you fill out our newsletter sign-up form.
* **Name (if provided):** Collected to personalise our communication with you.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit our website (see Section 3).

## 3. Website Analytics and Cookies
We use **Google Analytics 4**, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors find and use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies (small files stored on your device) and similar technologies to collect information such as:
* Pages visited and how you navigate between them
* Approximate location (for example, city or country), derived from your IP address
* Device, browser, and operating system type
* How you arrived at our site (for example, a search engine or link)

This information is used in aggregate to improve our website and is not used to identify you personally. It is processed by Google and may be stored on servers outside New Zealand, including in the United States.

Our website also embeds videos from **Vimeo** and loads fonts from **Google Fonts**. These providers may receive your IP address and may set their own cookies when this content loads. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the website display.

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We use your personal information strictly for the following purposes:
* To send you our newsletter, business updates, marketing materials, or promotional offers.
* To manage and maintain our subscriber distribution list.
* To analyse website usage (in aggregate) so we can improve our website and services.

## 5. How We Protect and Store Your Information
We take reasonable security steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. 
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for their marketing purposes. The service providers described in this policy (such as Google and Vimeo) handle information only as needed to provide their services.
* **Data Hosting:** Your data is securely stored within our website database or our secure email service provider platform.

## 6. Your Rights (Access and Correction)
Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:
* **Request a copy** of the personal information we hold about you.
* **Request correction** of your information if you believe it is inaccurate or out of date.
* **Opt-out or Unsubscribe** at any time. Every newsletter we send includes a functional, one-click 'Unsubscribe' link. We will process your opt-out request immediately and no later than 5 working days from submission.
